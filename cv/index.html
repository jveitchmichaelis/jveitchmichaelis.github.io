Professional Summary

Applied machine learning researcher specialising in remote sensing, with over a decade of software development experience. I have worked in academia and industry, from space science to ecology and I’m passionate about using technology for social good and planetary stewardship. I have wintered in Antarctica for two world-class telescopes (IceCube and SPT).
